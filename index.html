Infrastructure Backups

A personal-use utility for creating and recovering encrypted infrastructure backups in the account owner's Google Drive.

How it works

The utility uses Google Drive authorization to manage its own backup files. Backup content is encrypted before upload. This is not a public backup service, and no account registration is offered.
